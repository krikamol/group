Rational Intelligence Seminar Series (RISS)

The Rational Intelligence Seminar Series (RISS), seeks to advance the understanding of rationality, efficiency and reliability in machine learning systems. These seminars serve as a forum for discussions and quick dissemination of results.

RISS brings together researchers working on rationality, efficiency and reliability in machine learning, from uncertainty quantification and decision-making to evaluation and human–AI collaboration. If your work would be of interest to the RISS audience, we would love to hear from you at riss@ri-lab.org.
